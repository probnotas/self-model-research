What the run shows

  • Nothing tells it the motor changed. It works that out from the gap between what it predicted and what its body did.
  • It repairs itself from its own failures — the moves it made while falling over are the entire training set for the fix.
  • No reward function, no demonstrations, no physics given. The model came from 2,000 transitions of self-generated motion.

What it does not show

  • Balance-hold, not swing-up from hanging. Swing-up is beyond this planner, which is why it gets stood back up rather than righting itself.
  • The repair is not perfect: 8 of 10 runs balanced afterwards, against 10 of 10 on the healthy body.
  • It does not beat reinforcement learning here. A model-free policy trained on the healthy body shrugged off the same damage with no adaptation, holding 8 of 10 where this planner held 0 of 10. What it cannot do is tell you it is broken.
